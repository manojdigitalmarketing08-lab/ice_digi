What Is a Photo Booth?

A photo booth is a self-contained setup — a camera, a screen, and a printer or sharing tool — that lets guests capture a moment on the spot and walk away with it instantly. Some are enclosed booths, some are open backdrops, and some are just software running on a camera and screen you already own. Whatever the form, the idea stays the same: press a button, strike a pose, and leave with a real print or an instant digital share.
